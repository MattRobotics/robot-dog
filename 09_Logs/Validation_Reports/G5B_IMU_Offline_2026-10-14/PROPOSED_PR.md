Adds a standalone C++17 offline stabilization pipeline that reuses G5-A attitude monitoring, the bounded integral regulator and tilted contact IK. It accepts explicit nominal pose and confirmed contact evidence, applies roll/pitch corrections in Cartesian space, and publishes all 12 semantic joints atomically only after timing, IK, operational-bound and continuity checks.

This is **not a firmware release**. The module is outside the firmware source tree, has no actuator/backend access, and adds no runtime wiring. Moving support planes and tilt-rate damping are gated offline experiments. PR #47/#49, the driver, motion runtime, calibration, hardware configuration and canonical REV00 geometry are unchanged.

Validation: 811 C++ checks; 72 independent URDF/contact/SciPy cases; deterministic byte-identical replays; 12 synthetic control scenarios (7,200 samples); GCC strict C++17 and Clang ASan/UBSan; existing G1–G5-A host suites and historical FK/IK/gait oracles; 46 pertinent Python kinematics tests. The full old Python suite retains a **preexisting FAIL**: four visual-zero tests produce 16 errors because they expect an obsolete calibration-status value. The three relevant baseline files are byte-identical to main; they were not changed. The report preserves both `all_passed=false` and `required_gates_passed=true`.

The synthetic damping candidate settles more slowly than the baseline, and the periodic fixture mildly amplifies the error. No controller gain, latency or physical balancing claim is approved. Driver acquisition/quality/epoch access, observed support, collision/path/acceleration validation, end-to-end timing and REV01 inertial inputs remain future dependencies.
